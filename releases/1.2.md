## 中文

Poketoilet 1.2，适用于 Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge。

- 修复普通宝可梦携带帝王火龙果时，等待记录过早丢失而不触发的问题。
- 普通宝可梦完整在场经历一回合后触发；满级火属性宝可梦在入场动画结束后立即触发。
- 换下、倒下、失去道具或战斗结束时取消等待；重新上场重新计时。
- 火属性即时触发读取 Cobblemon 配置中的最高等级。
- 增加 19 项回合调度回归检查，更新中英文道具提示。

安装：将 **poketoilet-1.2.jar** 与 **cobblemon-ext-1.2.jar** 一同放入客户端和服务端的 `mods`，替换对应旧版本。另需自行安装 PoopSky、Cobblemon 和 Kotlin for Forge；本发布不包含这些依赖。

验证基线：Java 21、NeoForge 21.1.240、PoopSky 2.2+NeoForge1.21.1-Hotfix2、Cobblemon 1.7.3、Kotlin for Forge 5.12.0。其他组合尚未逐一验证。

提供 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 校验下载。自动测试覆盖回合调度和 Showdown 协议；普通首发伊布在游戏内验证了第二回合触发、道具消耗以及后续回合不重复触发。

## English

Poketoilet 1.2 for Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge.

- Fix ordinary Pokémon never activating King of Dragon Fruit because pending entries were discarded too early.
- Ordinary holders activate after one complete turn on the field; max-level Fire-type holders activate after their entry animation.
- Switching out, fainting, losing the item or ending the battle cancels the wait. Re-entry starts a new wait.
- Immediate Fire-type activation now respects Cobblemon's configured level cap.
- Add 19 turn-scheduling regression checks and update Chinese and English item tooltips.

Installation: put **poketoilet-1.2.jar** and **cobblemon-ext-1.2.jar** into `mods` on both client and server, replacing their older versions. Install PoopSky, Cobblemon and Kotlin for Forge separately; they are not included in this release.

Validation baseline: Java 21, NeoForge 21.1.240, PoopSky 2.2+NeoForge1.21.1-Hotfix2, Cobblemon 1.7.3 and Kotlin for Forge 5.12.0. Other combinations have not been individually verified.

Use `SHA256SUMS.txt` to verify downloads. Automated checks cover turn scheduling and the Showdown protocol. An in-game test with a lead Eevee confirmed turn-two activation, item consumption and no repeat activation on later turns.
